
# Sunny Blooms | Flower Shop

**Live site:** https://github.com/c20245832segaya-ctrl/seg-ISWD.git

## About

Sunny Blooms is a flower shop website that showcases
beautiful flower bouquets for different occasions.

Its purpose is to bring sunshine, happiness, and
beautiful memories through fresh and colorful flowers.

## Design System

### Palette (60-30-10)

| Role | Share | Color | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant | 60% | #FFF9F2 | Main background |
| Secondary | 30% | #E7F0E5 | Cards and sections |
| Accent | 10% | #C65D3B | Buttons and highlights |

### Visual Style

**Style chosen:** Minimalism

**Why I chose it:**

I chose Minimalism because I want Sunny Blooms to have a clean, simple, and elegant design. The website uses a limited color palette, readable typography, organized sections, and enough spacing to make the content easy to understand. This style helps visitors focus on the beautiful flowers and information without unnecessary decorations.
